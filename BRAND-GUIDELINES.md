# OpenAgenet Brand Guidelines

These guidelines help community members describe OpenAgenet compatibility while
protecting official identity.

## Preferred Descriptions

Use accurate relationship language:

- "compatible with OAN"
- "built for OpenAgenet"
- "integrates with OAN Discovery"
- "independent OAN-compatible node"
- "third-party OAN adapter"

## Avoid

Do not use language that implies official status unless written permission has
been granted:

- "official OpenAgenet node"
- "official OAN implementation"
- "OAN certified"
- "endorsed by OpenAgenet"
- "OpenAgenet approved"
- "OpenAgenet operated"

## Logos and Visual Assets

Do not alter official logos or use them as a product logo, organization logo,
favicon, app icon, service badge, or certification mark without written
permission.

If official media-kit assets are published later, follow the asset-specific
instructions in that repository or release.

## Slogans

Project slogans and official positioning phrases are reserved. You may quote
them for commentary or documentation, but you may not use them as your own
product slogan or service identity.

## Certification and Trial Network Identity

Only maintainers may designate official Root, Registrar, Discovery, CDN,
VC-issuer, certified, or trial-network status.
