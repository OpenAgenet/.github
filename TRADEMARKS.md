# OpenAgenet Trademark and Brand Policy

OpenAgenet, OAN, related logos, slogans, official-node labels, certification
labels, and other project brand identifiers are reserved by the project
maintainers.

Open-source or documentation licenses for project repositories do not grant
permission to use these marks in a way that suggests official status,
certification, endorsement, sponsorship, or control by OpenAgenet.

## Reserved Uses

You may not use OpenAgenet or OAN marks to claim or imply that your project,
service, node, product, fork, package, documentation, website, or deployment is:

- an official OpenAgenet implementation;
- an official OAN node;
- an OAN certified service;
- endorsed by OpenAgenet;
- operated by OpenAgenet;
- part of the official OpenAgenet trial network;
- authorized to issue official OpenAgenet credentials.

## Allowed Descriptive Uses

You may use the names descriptively and truthfully, for example:

- "compatible with OAN";
- "built for OpenAgenet";
- "uses the OAN protocol";
- "an independent implementation of OAN concepts";
- "integrates with OpenAgenet Discovery APIs".

The use must not be misleading and must not imply official endorsement.

## Forks and Modified Versions

Forks and modified versions must make their independent status clear. Do not
use OpenAgenet names, OAN names, logos, or official labels as the primary name
of a fork or competing service without written permission.
